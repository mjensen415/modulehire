# ModuleHire

## Product purpose
ModuleHire is a modular resume builder from ModuleHire Labs. People upload a resume once, it becomes a library of reusable skill and experience modules, and they build a tailored resume for each job from those modules. It is in public beta. ModuleHire Labs was founded in 2026 to help people present themselves better with AI.

## Users
Job seekers tailoring resumes, often under time pressure, on the web or from an AI assistant. A separate version serves hiring teams ranking applicants. Journalists and partners use the press kit to get accurate copy and assets quickly.

## Brand personality
Direct, capable, honest. Plain language. Never hype, and no claim the product can't back up.

## Register
Product and marketing share one visual system: dark navy by default with a light theme, Plus Jakarta Sans, teal for action, green in the logo.
