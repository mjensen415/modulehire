ONE-SENTENCE DESCRIPTION
ModuleHire turns one resume into a library of reusable modules, then builds a tailored resume for each job from what you've actually done.

SHORT DESCRIPTION
ModuleHire is a resume builder based on a simple idea: your experience should be reusable. Upload a resume once and ModuleHire breaks it into skill and experience modules. Paste a job posting and it builds a tailored resume from those modules, shows how well it covers the job, and lets you edit it line by line. It also connects to AI assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT, so you can do the same work from a chat.

LONG DESCRIPTION
ModuleHire is a resume builder from ModuleHire Labs, a company founded in 2026 to help people present themselves better with AI. Instead of rewriting a resume for every application, users upload it once and ModuleHire breaks it into a library of reusable skill and experience modules. Paste a job posting or a link, and ModuleHire picks the relevant modules, writes a tailored draft, and scores how well it covers each requirement. The draft opens as an editable document. Those modules are the source of facts: if an edit adds a number or a name its module doesn't contain, ModuleHire flags it for review. ModuleHire also connects to AI assistants through the Model Context Protocol, so users can save a new win, tailor a resume, or edit a line from a chat. A separate version helps hiring teams rank applicants against a job description. ModuleHire is in public beta, with a free plan available.
